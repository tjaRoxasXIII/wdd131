Steam Frame Overview

The Steam Frame is Valve’s wired VR and standalone virtual display hardware, designed to deliver immersive gaming experiences powered by SteamOS.

Specifications

CategoryDetails
DisplayHigh-resolution dual OLED panels
Refresh Rate90–120 Hz
Field of View~110 degrees
TrackingExternal sensors with wired connection
ConnectivityUSB-C, HDMI, wired VR link
Operating SystemSteamOS (Linux-based)
CompatibilityWorks with SteamVR titles and can even run your standalone library titles

Key Features

  • Immersive VR gaming with SteamVR support.
  • High refresh rate and wide field of view.
  • Wired connection for low latency and stable performance.
  • Compatible with SteamOS and Proton for cross-platform play.
  • Supports virtual desktop mode for productivity and media.
