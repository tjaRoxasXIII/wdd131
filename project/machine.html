Steam Machine Overview

The Steam Machine is Valve’s living room gaming PC, designed to bring the power of SteamOS into a console-style form factor for couch gaming.

Specifications

CategoryDetails
ProcessorCustom x86 CPU options (Intel/AMD depending on model)
GPUDiscrete NVIDIA/AMD graphics depending on configuration
RAM8–16 GB DDR4
Storage Options500 GB – 2 TB HDD/SSD
Operating SystemSteamOS (Linux-based)
ConnectivityWi-Fi, Ethernet, HDMI, USB ports
Form FactorConsole-style case for living room setup

Key Features

  • Console-style design for living room gaming.
  • Runs SteamOS with access to the full Steam library.
  • Supports controllers, keyboards, and mice.
  • Upgradeable hardware depending on manufacturer.
  • Optimized for big-screen play.
