Steam Hardware Hub

A central hub for Valve’s Steam hardware offerings.

Site Purpose

The site provides specifications, pricing, and store links for Valve’s Steam hardware devices.

Scenarios

  • What are the technical specifications of the Steam Deck compared to the new handheld?
  • Which hardware is best for travel?
  • How can I purchase the hardware?

Color Schema

Primary: Dark Gray (#1B1B1B) - backgrounds

Accent: Steam Blue (#1B9AAA) - headings, buttons

Secondary: Light Gray (#F5F5F5) - body text background

Typography

Headings: Roboto Condensed

Body: Open Sans

Accents: Orbitron
